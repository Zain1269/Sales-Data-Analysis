Sales Data Analysis: Customer, Product & Revenue Insights

Overview

A Python-based data analysis project focused on understanding sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, country-level sales, and monthly trends.

The project uses Pandas for data analysis and Matplotlib for data visualization.

Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
- VS Code
- strtamlit
- plotly

Analysis Areas

Sales Overview

Analyzed key business metrics including total sales, average sale value, quantity sold, invoices, customers, and products.

Product Analysis

Identified the top products by quantity and revenue and compared the two rankings.

Customer Analysis

Analyzed top-spending customers, average customer spending, and repeat customers.

Country Analysis

Compared sales performance across countries and identified the strongest markets.

Monthly Sales Analysis

Analyzed monthly sales to identify high and low-performing periods and overall sales fluctuations.

Key Insights

- The highest-revenue products differed from the most frequently purchased products.
- The United Kingdom generated more than 15 times the sales of the Netherlands.
- November 2011 recorded the highest sales in the available data.
- Sales fluctuated significantly across the analyzed period.
- December 2011 had incomplete data, covering only the first nine days.

Recommendations

1. Focus marketing efforts on the UK market.
2. Maintain sufficient stock of high-revenue products.
3. Investigate low-performing months.
4. Review the profitability of lower-performing markets.
